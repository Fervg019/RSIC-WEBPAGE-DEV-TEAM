Realms/10 Law & Sociology

Athena's Bench

Argue the case. Then argue the system.

Athena's Bench runs a moot-court structure with a sociological twist. Teams argue a case, then answer for the social assumptions baked into the law they're citing.

10
Theme

Moot court + critical argument

Format

Team moot + bench questioning

Team size

2 to 3

Duration

Full day

Rules

  1. Case brief and memorandum submitted before the moot.
  2. Speaking order and time limits are fixed and enforced.
  3. Bench questioning may interrupt submissions at any point.
  4. Citations must follow the provided format guide.
  5. Teams argue both sides across preliminary rounds.

Judging criteria

  • Legal argument30%
  • Bench responsiveness25%
  • Sociological critique25%
  • Advocacy20%

Requirements

  • Memorandum (submitted in advance)
  • Printed case bundle
  • Formal attire
  • Delegate ID

Schedule & materials

  • Full timings are released with the event schedule on the Updates page.
  • Room and stage assignments are confirmed the week of RSIC.

Heads upBeing unable to answer a bench question is scored more favourably than answering it incorrectly with confidence.
