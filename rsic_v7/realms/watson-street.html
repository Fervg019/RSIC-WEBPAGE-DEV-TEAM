Realms/03 Biology

The Watson Street

Living systems don't wait for you.

The Watson Street runs practical biology under a clock. Specimen identification, case-based diagnosis, and questions that punish memorised answers over actual understanding.

03
Theme

Applied biological reasoning

Format

Rotational stations

Team size

1 to 2

Duration

2.5 hours

Rules

  1. Each station has a fixed time limit. No carrying over.
  2. No phones or reference material at any station.
  3. Station order is assigned, not chosen.
  4. Partial credit given for documented reasoning, not just final answers.
  5. Contamination of specimens ends that station's attempt.

Judging criteria

  • Accuracy40%
  • Reasoning shown30%
  • Speed15%
  • Lab conduct15%

Requirements

  • Lab coat (provided if unavailable)
  • Closed-toe shoes
  • Delegate ID at each station
  • Pen only. No pencil for written responses

Schedule & materials

  • Full timings are released with the event schedule on the Updates page.
  • Room and stage assignments are confirmed the week of RSIC.

Heads upAny station involving live or preserved specimens follows a strict handling briefing given on arrival. Missing it means sitting that station out.
