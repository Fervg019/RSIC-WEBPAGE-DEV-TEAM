Realms/02 Pure Sciences

Project Nexus

Hypothesis in, evidence out.

Project Nexus is built around the scientific method itself. Designing an experiment, defending its logic, and surviving cross-examination from judges who've run the same experiment badly at least once.

02
Theme

Experimental design under scrutiny

Format

Team research + live defence

Team size

2 to 4

Duration

Full day

Rules

  1. Teams submit a written brief before the live defence.
  2. All data must be reproducible from the brief alone.
  3. Judges may ask follow-up questions for up to 10 minutes.
  4. External sources must be cited in the brief.
  5. One resubmission window before the deadline.

Judging criteria

  • Methodology35%
  • Evidence quality30%
  • Defence20%
  • Clarity15%

Requirements

  • Written brief (max 6 pages)
  • Any physical materials for demonstration
  • Laptop for slides if presenting digitally
  • Printed citation list

Schedule & materials

  • Full timings are released with the event schedule on the Updates page.
  • Room and stage assignments are confirmed the week of RSIC.

Heads upBriefs submitted after the stated deadline are marked but capped at 70% of the total score.
