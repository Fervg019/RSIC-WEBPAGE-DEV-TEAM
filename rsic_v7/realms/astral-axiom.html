Realms/05 Physics

Astral Axiom

The universe doesn't grade on effort.

Astral Axiom is a physics gauntlet. Mechanics, waves and a healthy dose of astrophysics, tested through problems that reward a correct mental model more than a memorised formula.

05
Theme

Conceptual physics under pressure

Format

Individual written + rapid-fire round

Team size

1

Duration

3 hours

Rules

  1. Formula sheet provided. No personal formula sheets.
  2. Rapid-fire round is timed per question, no going back.
  3. Calculators must be non-programmable.
  4. Partial working is scored even on incorrect final answers.
  5. Any electronic device other than a calculator is confiscated for the round.

Judging criteria

  • Written round60%
  • Rapid-fire round30%
  • Working shown10%

Requirements

  • Non-programmable scientific calculator
  • Delegate ID
  • Pen and pencil
  • Own ruler if diagrams are expected

Schedule & materials

  • Full timings are released with the event schedule on the Updates page.
  • Room and stage assignments are confirmed the week of RSIC.

Heads upThe rapid-fire round is scored live. Scores are visible on-screen as the round progresses.
